# CHALÉ LIFE — Middle-Ground Character Style Bible

**Official Visual Identity (Locked)**

> A stylized blocky Ghanaian life simulator that feels like a second life in Accra — simplified and readable like a high-quality Roblox experience, but grounded and personal enough that the character feels like *you*, not a toy.

---

## 1. Core Principle

The player must feel: **“This is my second life in Accra.”**

Not: “This is a cute Roblox avatar with Ghanaian clothes.”

---

## 2. Proportions (Strict)

| Measurement              | Value                          | Notes |
|--------------------------|--------------------------------|-------|
| Total height             | 1.70 – 1.76 m                  | Adult range |
| Head-to-body ratio       | **1 : 5.0**                    | Slightly larger than real life, far smaller than classic Roblox |
| Head size                | ~0.30 m cube                   | Readable but human |
| Eye diameter             | **0.028 – 0.032 m**            | Medium human eyes — never giant white spheres |
| Limb thickness           | Chunky but human               | Upper arm ~0.12–0.13 m, thigh ~0.14–0.15 m |
| Hand / Foot              | Simplified boxes               | Clear silhouette, not tiny |

---

## 3. Face Rules

- Eyes: Medium size, warm dark-brown iris, subtle highlight. Human proportion.
- No oversized cartoon eyes.
- Mouth: Simple, neutral-to-slight smile. Not exaggerated.
- Nose: Minimal or implied (blocky geometry does not need a detailed nose).
- Ears: Simple block or slightly rounded.
- Expression must feel like a real person living in Accra.

---

## 4. Body & Geometry

- Primary shapes: Boxes with soft visual weight (no ultra-thin limbs).
- Torso: Slightly wider than tall, clear silhouette.
- Clothing: Solid base colors + one strong Ghanaian accent (Kente stripe, Ankara panel, smock pattern).
- Hair: Simplified block styles that still read as Ghanaian (fade, twists, bun, kufi).
- No hyper-detailed sculpting. No pure Lego stud aesthetic.

---

## 5. Color & Material

- Skin: Warm Ghanaian tones only (`#5C3317`, `#6B3E26`, `#4A2511`, `#7A482B`).
- Clothing: Contemporary Accra streetwear + cultural accents.
- Materials: Slight roughness (0.5–0.7). No shiny plastic look.
- Lighting response must feel warm coastal Accra sunlight.

---

## 6. Strictly Forbidden

- Giant Roblox-style white eyes
- Pure Lego minifigure proportions
- Hyper-realistic faces or uncanny textures
- Generic Western life-sim blandness
- Mixing highly detailed world assets with toy-like characters without intention
- Any asset that makes the player feel like they are controlling a toy instead of living a second life

---

## 7. Quality Gate (Before Accepting Any Character)

Ask every time:

1. Does this feel like a real Ghanaian person living in Accra?
2. Would I want this to be *my* second-life avatar?
3. Are the eyes human-sized?
4. Is the head only slightly larger than realistic?
5. Does the silhouette stay clear and readable?
6. Does it still look blocky/simplified enough for consistent AI generation?

If any answer is “no” → reject and revise.
